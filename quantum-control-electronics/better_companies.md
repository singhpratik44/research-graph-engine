## WHAT MAKES A "BETTER" QUANTUM COMPANY?

**Technical Excellence**:
- [ ] Published papers in peer-reviewed journals (Nature, Science, PRX Quantum, arXiv)
- [ ] Demonstrated benchmarks (error rates, qubit counts, gate fidelities)
- [ ] Published roadmap (50→500→5000 qubits with timeline)
- [ ] Real-time control focus (not just algorithms)
- [ ] Error correction work (not hand-wavy)

**Business Stability**:
- [ ] $50M+ funding (can survive 3-5 years without exit)
- [ ] Diverse funding sources (VC + government contracts)
- [ ] Public company or late-stage private (less risk)
- [ ] Enterprise customers (validates business model)
- [ ] Experienced management (ex-tech/physics/business leaders)

**Team Quality**:
- [ ] PhD advisors from top universities (MIT, Stanford, Yale, UC Berkeley)
- [ ] Low turnover (LinkedIn history shows stability)
- [ ] Diverse team (physics + engineering + business)
- [ ] Published researchers (not just papers, they're authors)

**Culture & Opportunity**:
- [ ] Competitive salary + stock ($250k-$350k for research scientist)
- [ ] Publication culture (allowed to publish research)
- [ ] Work-life balance (quantum is hard, but not sweatshop)
- [ ] Growth path (can become principal/staff)
- [ ] Learning budget (conferences, courses)
