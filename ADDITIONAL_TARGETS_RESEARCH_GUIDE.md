## Key Insights: Enterprise Tier vs. Startup Tier

### Startup Tier (Atom, IonQ, D-Wave, PsiQuantum, Google Quantum AI)

**Advantages**:
- Cutting-edge quantum hardware/algorithms
- Closer to core quantum systems work
- Potential for significant equity upside
- Smaller, more focused teams

**Challenges**:
- Often require on-site presence
- Potentially less stable
- May have narrower focus (just quantum, not enterprise)

**Best For**: If you want pure quantum engineering, equity, and research

### Enterprise Tier (AWS, Palo Alto, NXP, IBM)

**Advantages**:
- Massive scale and infrastructure
- Quantum is *part* of larger systems (hybrid classical-quantum)
- Strong PQC adoption (regulatory pressure, enterprise customers)
- Program/project management opportunities (not just engineering)
- Remote-friendly, stable, global

**Challenges**:
- Bureaucracy and slower decision-making
- Quantum team may be smaller within large org
- May focus more on adoption than cutting-edge research

**Best For**: If you want scale, stability, hybrid systems experience, and program leadership

### Recommendation

**Apply to Both Tiers**:
- 5-7 applications to startup tier (control engineer, simulator engineer roles)
- 8-10 applications to enterprise tier (architect, program manager, infrastructure roles)
- This gives you optionality: startup equity vs. enterprise scale
